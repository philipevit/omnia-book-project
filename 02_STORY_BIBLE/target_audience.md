# Target Audience

OMNIA is written for modern individuals who have internalized society's dominant definition of success and achievement, yet increasingly feel that something essential about life may have been lost along the way.

The ideal reader is not defined primarily by profession, age, or demographics, but by a specific psychological state.

The ideal reader is someone who:

* constantly feels pressure to perform
* has learned to associate self-worth with achievement
* struggles to slow down or disconnect from productivity
* often prioritizes external success over inner wellbeing
* has quietly neglected health, relationships, presence, or meaning
* feels restless despite outward progress
* increasingly questions whether modern society's definition of success is fundamentally incomplete

Primary audiences include:

* entrepreneurs and founders
* corporate professionals
* executives and managers
* ambitious knowledge workers
* high performing professionals
* productivity driven individuals
* people deeply immersed in achievement culture

Secondary audiences include readers interested in:

* psychology
* personal growth
* mindfulness
* mental health
* philosophy
* human behavior
* modern wellbeing
* life design

The perfect reader finishes the book feeling personally challenged by one central question:

*"Have I spent years building a successful life while quietly losing connection to what actually makes life meaningful?"*
