# Protagonist Foundation

The protagonist of OMNIA represents the modern high-performing individual who has fully internalized society's dominant definition of success.

He has spent years mastering everything modern culture teaches people to pursue.

He has learned how to build external success through discipline, ambition, achievement, productivity, financial progress, status, self-development, and relentless personal optimization.

From the outside, he appears to have figured life out.

He is respected, disciplined, intelligent, physically healthy, financially secure, highly capable, and admired by others.

Yet beneath the surface, something essential has quietly begun disappearing.

Years of constantly pursuing growth, optimization, achievement, and external progress have gradually disconnected him from deeper human needs such as emotional connection, presence, spontaneity, inner peace, fulfillment, and genuine meaning.

The deeper irony of the character lies at the very center of the story.

The more he has mastered the art of optimizing his life…

…the further he has drifted away from the very experience of simply being alive.

The protagonist does not simply represent ambition, entrepreneurship, or professional success.

He represents modern humanity's obsession with constant improvement and external achievement.

He is the embodiment of a culture that has become exceptionally sophisticated at improving life…

…while gradually forgetting how to truly live.
