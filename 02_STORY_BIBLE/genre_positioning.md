# Genre Positioning

OMNIA is a work of transformational commercial fiction designed to combine emotionally engaging storytelling with deep psychological insight and meaningful philosophical reflection.

The book is not intended to function as traditional self-help, science fiction, literary fiction, or genre fiction.

Instead, OMNIA belongs to a modern category of narrative experiences that entertain readers while simultaneously challenging them to reflect on the way they live their own lives.

The story uses fiction as a vehicle for personal transformation.

Its purpose is not simply to tell an interesting story.

Its purpose is to create emotional recognition, internal reflection, and lasting perspective shifts within the reader.

Comparable positioning includes works such as:

* The Alchemist
* The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari
* Achtsam morden
* The Midnight Library

The writing style should remain highly accessible, emotionally resonant, intellectually stimulating, and commercially readable for a broad audience.

The ultimate objective is creating a transformational reading experience disguised as an entertaining novel.
