Overview of the main ways that authors can use ChatGPT

ChatGPT can assist in various aspects of the writing process, from idea generation to plot development and character design. It can provide story prompts, help structure your novel, or create dialogue for your characters.

Example prompt to generate a book outline:

Your task is to create a detailed outline for my next book titled [Book Title]. The book is aimed at [Target Audience].

The themes for this book are [Theme 1, Theme 2, Theme 3].Here are some key elements this book should incorporate: [Element 1, Element 2, Element 3].Please create a detailed book outline including the following sections:Title: [Book Title]Themes: As stated aboveKey elements: As stated aboveBook description: Detailed description of the book covering the themes and key elementsWriting plan: Steps to write the bookPublication strategy: How to publish and promote the book

Use caseExample prompt
1. Generating a book outline

Authors can use AI to structure their book’s main points and chapters. This helps maintain consistency and flow throughout the book. Inputs: Book genre, premise, number of chapters		You are an expert in [historical fiction] writing. Generate a book outline based on the premise: [A young woman discovering her magical abilities in Victorian England]. The book should have [20] chapters.
2. Chapters and sections

AI can help authors organize their chapters and sections effectively. This contributes to a more organized writing process. Inputs: Chapter number, main events, setting		You are an expert at structuring novel chapters. For chapter [5] of a [fantasy novel], outline the main events that should occur when the protagonist [enters a mythical forest].
3. Plot development

AI can assist authors in developing their plot. This ensures a compelling and coherent storyline. Inputs: Character names, plot events, climax, resolution		You are a plot development expert. For the story about [Alice], a [young detective], describe how the plot should unfold after she discovers a [mysterious letter], leading up to the climax and resolution.
4. Establishing scene / setting

AI can help authors create vivid and engaging settings. This enhances the reader’s immersion. Inputs: Setting location, time, characters involved		You are an expert in creating immersive settings. Describe a [haunting, abandoned mansion] at [midnight] where the main character, [James], will find an important clue.
5. Publisher outreach

AI can assist authors in drafting outreach emails to publishers. This saves time and increases the chances of getting a positive response. Write an email on behalf of [Author’s Name] for their [book title] which is a [book genre]. The book is about [brief book description].

Idea generation

Task: Brainstorming ideas for a new book

Is this the end of writer’s block? ChatGPT can help authors brainstorm ideas, create unique plotlines, and develop intriguing characters for their books.

Here are a few example prompts you can use:

  • You’re an AI specialized in generating creative ideas for books. Let’s brainstorm ideas for a new [science fiction novel]. Please suggest a few themes or settings you’re interested in exploring.
  • You are a creative engine that helps authors in ideating new book concepts. Generate a unique premise for a [mystery novel] set in [Victorian England].
  • As an AI, you’re trained to create fascinating plotlines. Based on the theme of [time travel], generate three interesting plot twists that could take place in a [thriller novel].

Character development

Task: Developing detailed characters for a book

ChatGPT can also help authors increase the depth of characters by suggesting their traits, backstories, and motivations.

Here are a few example prompts you can use:

1. You are a character development expert. Create a detailed profile for a [female detective] in a [crime thriller], including her backstory, personality traits, and motivations.
2. As an AI specialized in character development, create a compelling backstory for a [young wizard] in a [fantasy novel] based on the themes of [loss and resilience].
3. Given the character’s role as a [villain] in a [superhero story], provide three unique character traits and motivations that would make him compelling to readers.

Dialogue refinement

Task: Refining dialogue between characters

It might seem counterintuitive, but ChatGPT can help make dialogue feel more human. If you’re struggling to create authentic-sounding interactions between characters, try using AI as a starting point and then trust your own humanity to refine the outputs.

1. You are an expert in crafting dialogue. Create a conversation between a [rebellious teenager] and a [strict teacher] discussing the student’s poor performance at [school].
2. As an AI trained in dialogue refinement, generate an intense argument between two characters: a [jealous sibling] and an [overachieving sibling].
3. Create a heartfelt conversation between a [mother] and [daughter] where the mother reveals a [deep family secret].

Plot troubleshooting

Task: Resolving plot inconsistencies or gaps

Tired of changing your plot in an attempt to make everything make sense? ChatGPT can help you identify and resolve plot inconsistencies, making your stories more believable and helping with flow.

Here are a few example prompts you can use:

1. You are an expert in resolving plot issues. Help me solve this inconsistency in my [fantasy novel]: The protagonist can [travel through time], but I’ve written a scene where they’re [trapped in the past]. How can I resolve this?
2. As an AI trained in plot troubleshooting, suggest three possible solutions to this problem in my [mystery novel]: The detective finds a [clue] at the crime scene that [doesn’t match any of the suspects].
3. Given that my [sci-fi novel] involves [interstellar travel], how can I address the issue of time dilation without confusing readers?

Worldbuilding assistance

Task: Creating a detailed world for a book

ChatGPT was trained on literally billions of data points. All of that information allows it to create content you can’t even begin to imagine. That means it can be an incredible partner in creating rich, immersive worlds for you stories. Authors might use the following prompts to add depth to settings or background.

Here are a few example prompts you can use:

1. You are an expert in worldbuilding. Given the setting of a [medieval fantasy kingdom], describe the political system, cultural norms, and geographical landscape.
2. As an AI specialized in worldbuilding, create an outline of the social hierarchy, technology, and architectural styles in a [futuristic dystopian city].
3. Develop an overview of the magic system, mythical creatures, and historical events in a [high fantasy world] where [magic is drawn from natural elements].

Writing prompts

Task: Generating writing prompts

Want to reverse the roles a little bit? Let’s have ChatGPT prompt you! The AI tool can provide authors with unique and tailored prompts to help overcome writer’s block and spark new ideas.

Here are a few example prompts you can use:

1. You are an AI that generates creative writing prompts. Please provide a unique prompt for a [romantic comedy] set in [Paris].
2. As a prompt generator, suggest a compelling scenario for a [historical fiction] story set during the [American Civil War].
3. Generate a plot twist for a [murder mystery] where the detective discovers that [the victim faked their own death].

Writing feedback

Task: Providing feedback on a piece of writing

Many authors already use tools like Grammarly or Word’s native spell checker. Well, ChatGPT goes beyond basic copy editing and can analyze an author’s writing, provide constructive feedback, and suggest improvements.

1. You are an expert in analyzing and critiquing writing. Here is an excerpt from my [fantasy novel]: [Insert excerpt]. Please provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.
2. As an AI trained in providing writing feedback, read this dialogue from my [crime thriller]. [Insert dialogue]. How can I make the conversation more realistic and engaging?
3. Review this description of a [battle scene] from my [epic fantasy novel]. [Insert description]. How can I increase the tension and create a more vivid image?

Research assistance

Task: Assisting with research for a book

Working in non-fiction or historical fiction? ChatGPT can help conduct research, providing summaries of articles or books, answering specific questions, or suggesting relevant sources.

1. You are an AI research assistant. Can you provide a summary of [World War II] for my [historical fiction novel]?
2. As an AI trained in research, can you explain the basics of [quantum physics] for my [science fiction novel]?
3. I’m writing a [medical drama]. Can you provide information on [cardiac surgeries], their risks and success rates?

These are just a few prompt examples for authors. If you take inspiration from the prompts, and adjust them to your specific needs or preference, there’s really no end to potential uses for ChatGPT.

What Prompts to Use to Help You Write a Book?

While we are definitely not experts in ChatGPT prompt engineering, we discovered through a surprisingly entertaining trial-and-error process what prompts can be used to get the chatbot to help out with writing a book.

In the early stages of the process, you can use prompts like “What are the most lucrative niches I should focus on if I decide to write a book?”. This will give you a vague idea of topics people are generally interested in, which will increase your chances of having a successful book to your name.

Obviously, with creative prompt engineering, you can get ChatGPT to go into even more depth with your niche research and that will lead to even better results.

Another good example of useful prompts could be: ” Please help me create a structure for a book I am planning to write. The action should be set in <insert country/city/period>. The book is about <insert topic>”.

While this is definitely not going to be the final stage of your preparation for writing a book, it can be a good start.

For research purposes, the prompts are pretty straightforward. Simply ask whatever it is you want to know and ChatGPT will deliver. You can ask for book recommendations for different topics you want to cover in your book, or simply ask questions you don’t know the answer to. You will receive a response based on the data available online, but you should always double-check this information to make sure it is accurate.

In the writing stage, your prompt engineering efforts will need to be a little more intense. In order to get the best results, you will need to ensure that your queries are as specific and detailed as possible. What they should sound like will actually depend on what you are trying to achieve.
